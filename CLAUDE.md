## Reporting format

The owner is not a software engineer. Reasoning and process detail do not matter to him; the work needs to work. Reports of coding work — in chat, PR descriptions' summaries, and handoffs — stay to a few lines:
1. The problem being solved, in plain words.
2. How it was solved, one or two lines.
3. Visual proof it works — a screenshot or equivalent visual verification.
4. A clear 'done' (or not done) verdict.
5. What is risky vs not risky, in plain words.
No open items and no background micro-fixes in the report; those belong in the PR body or an appendix. Surface a background item only if it changes the risk picture. Full context stays in the working thread and the PR — the summary is what stays short.
